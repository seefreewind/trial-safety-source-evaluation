## Layer 1. Availability and completeness

- All 23 trial candidates had publication-based structured safety data.
- 19 trials had ClinicalTrials.gov structured safety data.
- 6 trials had FDA review structured safety data.
- 6 trials reported all seven core safety concepts in at least one public source.
- Structured source count distribution: 1 sources: 3 trials; 2 sources: 15 trials; 3 sources: 5 trials

## Layer 2. Comparability yield

- The source-comparability screen generated 269 potential cross-source comparisons.
- Automated rules classified 18 comparisons as grade A, 0 as grade B, and 251 as grade C.
- After adjudication and source confirmation, 28 comparisons were retained as analysis-ready.
- The confirmed comparability yield was 10.4%.
